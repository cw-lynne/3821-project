Experiment log
==============

Every test or benchmark run, with what the script does, what it is meant to
test, and the result. Newest entries at the bottom.

Board size is rows x 10 columns unless stated. Seeds 0-(games-1). "Cap" is the
piece limit per game. Scripts in this folder add ../tetris-base to the import
path, so they can be run from anywhere, e.g.
    python3 experiments/merge_vs_no_merge.py
Commands that use benchmark.py or main.py are run from tetris-base/.

Entries 1-10 were written up after the runs, on 2026-11-17, from this
session's results. Entries from 11 onwards were logged as they were run.

6. Sum vs final scoring
   Date:    2026-11-17
   Command: python3 benchmark.py --games 100 --settings 1x8:sum 1x8:final
            2x2:sum 2x2:final 2x8:sum 2x8:final 2x32:sum 2x32:final 3x8:sum
            3x8:final --csv modes.csv
            (Run while sum was still the default, as "1x8 1x8:final 2x2 ...".
            The modes are written out here so the command gives the same
            settings with the current default.)
   Does:    Plays each setting with both score modes on the same 100 seeds,
            5-row board, cap 5000, and saves every game to a CSV for a
            per-seed comparison.
   Tests:   Whether final scoring beats sum scoring in general, not just at 2x8.
   Result:  final won at every setting (sum -> final): 1x8 101.3 -> 133.0,
            2x2 132.9 -> 288.1, 2x8 481.9 -> 792.4, 2x32 520.5 -> 787.9,
            3x8 1406.3 -> 1709.0. Per seed, final cleared more on 55-71 of
            100 seeds. Final became the default.

7. Stack height per setting
   Script:  experiments/stack_height.py 5 100 2000   (args: rows games cap)
   Date:    2026-11-17
   Does:    Plays control, 1x8, 2x8 and 3x8 (sum and final) on 100 seeds,
            5-row board, cap 2000, recording column heights after every move.
   Tests:   Whether stronger settings keep the stack lower (the idea that a
            low board is an emergent trait of the best settings).
   Result:  Average column height falls as settings get stronger
            (control 1.99 -> 3x8 sum 1.65), but final keeps slightly higher
            stacks than sum while clearing more lines.

8. Evaluation function statistics
   Script:  experiments/eval_stats.py
   Date:    2026-11-17
   Does:    Plays plain-AI games on 5, 10 and 20-row boards and scores every
            legal placement each turn, recording each evaluation term.
   Tests:   The range, mean and makeup of Misakamm's evaluation scores.
   Result:  Chosen-move scores are always negative (e.g. 5 rows: mean

9. Duplicate board frequency
   Script:  experiments/duplicate_frequency.py
   Date:    2026-11-17
   Does:    Plays 40 games each at 1x8 and 2x8 (final scoring, 5-row board,
            cap 1500) and, at every search step after the first piece, counts
            lines whose board duplicates another line's board.
   Tests:   How often beam search actually merges duplicate boards.
   Result:  1x8: 2.1% of lines are duplicates, 2x8: 1.5%; an unmerged top 8
            would hold 7.65 / 7.63 distinct boards on average; 14.8% / 18.3%
            of steps have a duplicate in the top 8. (Run with the default
            score mode, final; the script now sets final explicitly.)

11. Merging vs not merging duplicate boards
   Script:  experiments/merge_vs_no_merge.py
   Date:    2026-11-17
   Does:    Plays 1x8, 2x2, 2x8 and 3x8 (final scoring) on the same 100 seeds,
            5-row board, cap 5000, once with the normal beam search and once
            with a copy whose merge step is removed (it just keeps the top K
            lines, duplicates included). Reports mean lines, 95% CI, median,
            ms/move, capped games and per-seed wins.
   Tests:   Whether merging duplicate boards actually makes the AI stronger.
   Result:  merged vs unmerged mean lines (per seed: merging better / worse;
            two-sided sign test p):
            1x8 133.0 vs 131.6 (1 / 1, same on 98 seeds);
            2x2 288.1 vs 209.6 (57 / 30, p = 0.005);
            2x8 792.4 vs 830.8 (39 / 53, p = 0.17, CIs overlap);
            3x8 1709.0 vs 1739.2 (34 / 38, p = 0.72; ~72% of games capped).
            Merging helps a lot at narrow beam width and makes no significant
            difference at width 8. Time per move is the same either way.
